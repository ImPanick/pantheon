The id becomes part of every tool name as mcp__homeassistant__<tool>. A double underscore anywhere in it breaks the one split("__", 2) that parses the namespaced name, routing the call to the wrong server. Unreachable today because ids are uuid4-derived — the moment you let people name servers, this field has to hold that invariant.
